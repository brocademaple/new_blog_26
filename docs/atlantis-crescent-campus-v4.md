# Atlantis Gallery v4 — Crescent Campus

## Layout contract

The new site is organized around an open underwater sculpture garden. There is no central palace and no mirrored pair of side buildings.

- Overall blockout envelope: approximately `95 m x 70 m`.
- Three terrain bands: arrival `Z=0`, garden loop `Z=3.2 m`, upper reef galleries `Z=6.4 m`.
- Central sculpture garden: open oval, approximately `28 m x 20 m`; keep the main sun shaft unobstructed.
- Left gallery: one long glass-vault hall, approximately `34 m x 9 m x 7 m`, embedded into the reef.
- Right galleries: three distinct pavilions, each approximately `12 m x 10 m`, stepped with the terrain.
- Archive: compact circular pavilion, approximately `14 m` diameter, offset toward the rear-left.
- Arrival: lower-left terrace; the first view should reveal the garden before the archive.

## Circulation

- Primary route: one continuous `3.2 m` wide loop around the central garden.
- Secondary routes: two `2.4 m` wide branches to reef overlooks and small galleries.
- Connect all three height bands with visible stairs and ramps; target a maximum ramp slope of `1:12`.
- Keep the outer service route behind the reef-facing gallery walls and outside the visitor loop.
- Every exhibition room must have a real floor, enclosed wall surface for artworks, and a clear doorway back to the loop.

## Architectural modules

Reuse and refine the existing column, archway, floor-tile and relief-panel components. Add only four new modular families for the v4 blockout:

1. Curved retaining-wall segment.
2. Glass-vault bay for the long gallery.
3. Terraced pavilion bay for the right reef.
4. Curved ramp and balustrade segment.

Keep pale limestone as the dominant mass, dark teal stone at foundations, oxidized bronze on ribs and edges, and warm neutral light inside protected gallery rooms.

## Camera and composition gates

- Hero camera: three-quarter view from the lower-left arrival terrace; do not center the archive pavilion.
- Plan camera: 60-degree aerial view showing the entire loop and all gallery entrances.
- Visitor camera: eye level on the loop, with the sculpture garden on one side and visible protected art on the other.
- Reject any blockout that reads as a central palace with two mirrored wings, a straight ceremonial avenue, or detached buildings on an empty seabed.
